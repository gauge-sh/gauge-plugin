description: Summarize how the user's brand shows up in AI search answers and what is driving it
---

Use the Gauge connector (see the `gauge-chat` skill) to report on the user's AI
search visibility. If the user named an organization, category, competitor, or
date range, use it; otherwise ask which organization and category, and default
to the last 30 days.

1. Read the category overview and the visibility trend for the period. Report
   non-branded (`visibility`) prompts unless the user asks for branded ones.
2. Compare against the main competitors.
3. Find the topics where the brand gained or lost the most, and the domains
   and pages that are cited most for them. Quote one or two answers that show
   the pattern.
4. Close with up to three concrete recommendations. Offer to add them to the
   Action Center (see the `gauge-chat` skill); do not create content
   without asking.
